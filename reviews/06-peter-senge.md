### 1. Observe Deeply (Systems Thinking Facts)
- **Elimination of "Fixes that Fail" Dynamic**: Slide 5 completely eliminates the dangerous policy that allowed dock supervisors to unilaterally downgrade and reroute purchase orders. This stops the vicious bullwhip cycle where short-term supplier penalization destabilized vendor forecasting and triggered multi-tier supply chain collapse.
- **Root Cause Problem-Solving**: Replaces adversarial vendor scorecards with collaborative supplier root-cause reviews and joint forecasting data sharing.
- **Mental Model Shift**: Shifts organizational perception from "blaming delinquent suppliers" to recognizing systemic delays, transit variability, and upstream scheduling lead-time misalignments.
