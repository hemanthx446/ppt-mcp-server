### 1. Observe Deeply (Interactive Design Facts)
- **Problem Dissolution over Doing the Wrong Thing Righter**: Slide 7 (Phase 4) and Slide 8 now introduce Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI) and automated gate scan clearances for certified Tier-1 suppliers, dissolving the need for manual dock receiving rather than merely automating paper clipboards onto tablets.
- **Frontline Stakeholder Co-Design**: Slide 7 (Phase 1) and Slide 8 allocate dedicated budget ($80,000) for frontline dock worker co-design clinics. The actual receiving clerks and forklift operators participate in designing their tools, preventing technocratic imposition.
- **Systemic Purpose**: The primary system objective is redefined from "tracking vendor infractions" to "ensuring uninterrupted human and machine flow across the plant."

### 2. Question Gently (10 Signature Questions)
1. **Idealized System Design**: Redesigns receiving workflows around automated trust and VMI.
2. **Dissolving Problems vs Fixing Symptoms**: Dissolves receiving bottlenecks for certified high-volume parts.
3. **Frontline Participation**: Frontline workers are active co-designers, not passive subjects.
4. **Doing the Right Thing**: Focuses on material flow into production, not just digitizing dock bureaucracy.
5. **Systemic Purpose Alignment**: Subordinates dock processes to overall manufacturing throughput.
6. **Eliminating Unnecessary Steps**: Automated ASN match bypasses manual purchase order cross-referencing.
7. **Stakeholder Lived Reality**: Respects the physical constraints and dignity of dock workers and drivers.
8. **Interconnected Whole vs Disconnected Parts**: Connects suppliers, dock, and factory floor into one system.
9. **Continuous Redesign Capability**: Architecture allows agile refinement of UI and workflow rules.
10. **Wisdom over Data**: Transforms transactional data into actionable operational calm.

### 3. Reveal Patiently (Structural Evaluation)
The revised architecture embraces Ackoff's profound maxim: "It is much better to do the right thing wrong than to do the wrong thing right." By incorporating VMI and frontline co-design, the solution avoids digitizing waste and instead dissolves receiving friction.
