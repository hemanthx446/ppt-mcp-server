## 1. Engagement Background & Objective

Jharkhand Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited (JBVNL) seeks to unlock the business, operational, and financial value of its existing SAP enterprise investment. While SAP has been implemented, daily operations across circles continue to rely on parallel manual registers, offline spreadsheets, and disconnected paperwork.

The primary objective of this 90-day engagement is to **eliminate parallel manual processes, standardize core procurement and inventory transactions in SAP, and establish real-time executive visibility in two pilot electric supply circles (Ranchi Central and Jamshedpur)**, thereby demonstrating quantifiable financial and working capital recovery prior to statewide scaling.

---

## 2. Detailed Scope of Work (SOW)

Lumbini Elite Solutions & Services shall deliver the following services across the three phases:

### Stream 1: Diagnostic & Transaction Audit (Days 1–30)
1. **Forensic Transaction Analysis:** Audit user logs (ST03N, SM20, CDHDR) across all 15 circles to establish the audited baseline adoption rate and identify specific transaction dropout points.
2. **Master Data Health Diagnostic:** Perform duplicate and consistency audits across Material Master (focusing on distribution transformers, cables, conductors), Vendor Master (GSTIN and MSME status), and Asset Master (CWIP line items).
3. **Process Bottleneck Mapping:** Document offline paper workflows and approval delays between circle field offices, circle stores, and central accounts.
4. **Deliverable D1:** *JBVNL Enterprise Baseline & Diagnostic Milestone Report (DMR)* submitted to the Project Steering Committee.

### Stream 2: Process Standardization & User Enablement (Days 31–60)
1. **Workflow Standardization:** Configure and test standard SAP digital approval workflows for purchase requisitions and purchase orders, enforcing Budget Availability Control (AVAC) limits.
2. **Master Data Cleansing Execution:** Execute approved deduplication and mass maintenance scripts for material and vendor masters in Ranchi Central and Jamshedpur.
3. **Screen Optimization:** Configure simplified, role-based SAP Fiori / WebGUI transaction tiles for storekeepers and junior engineers.
4. **Hands-On User Enablement:** Conduct classroom and simulated transaction training for 45 circle personnel; provide laminated standard operating procedure (SOP) quick-reference manuals.
5. **Deliverable D2:** *Pilot Readiness Certificate & Cleansed Master Data Sign-Off (D2)* approved by Superintending Engineers of Ranchi Central and Jamshedpur.

### Stream 3: Pilot Deployment, Exception Dashboard & Value Realization (Days 61–90)
1. **Pilot Cutover Execution:** Supervise the retirement of paper store chits and offline purchase approvals in Ranchi Central and Jamshedpur stores; activate 100% digital goods receipt (MIGO) and goods issue.
2. **Floor-Walking Support:** Deploy dedicated on-site functional specialists at Ranchi Central and Jamshedpur circle stores to assist field operators with live daily transactions.
3. **Executive Exception Cockpit:** Deploy a real-time management dashboard on the Managing Director and Director (Finance) desktops tracking daily store issues, pending approvals, and unlinked receipts.
4. **Financial Impact Audit:** Measure and verify the hard financial improvements achieved over 30 days of live operation (inventory holding reduction, purchase contract variance reduction, and CWIP capitalization dossiers).
5. **Deliverable D3:** *Final Pilot Value Realization Report (FVR) & Statewide Rollout Roadmap* presented to the Chairman and Board of Directors.
