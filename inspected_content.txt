    - [TextBox 15 / TEXT_BOX (17)]: Master data

Controls in SAP GRC Process Control can be used as mitigation controls in SAP GRC Access Control and vice versa;
Processes in SAP GRC Process Control can be used as business processes in SAP GRC Access Control.
The shared Risk Catalog is by SAP GRC Process Control and SAP GRC Risk Management. There is application-specific information for SAP GRC Process Control and SAP GRC Access Control applications
