### Integrity Safeguards & Compliance Boundary Notes

1. **Proposal vs. Delivery Safeguard:** Hical Technologies is strictly designated on Slide 6 as an *Executive Proposal & Target Architecture Blueprint*, preventing any misrepresentation of proposed scope as finished delivery.
2. **Entity Separation:** Elitia Technologies (proprietary microservices) and Lumbini Elite Solutions (consulting & enterprise services) are clearly defined as complementary capabilities under Lumbini Elite Group.
3. **No Fabricated Commercials:** The presentation contains zero unverified pricing, fixed SLA guarantees, or invented ROI percentages. All cited metrics (e.g., TEPL 70% confirmation reduction, month-end close from 10 to 4 days) are derived directly from verified client closure reports.
4. **Confidentiality:** Customer credentials, proprietary drawings, and sensitive financial rates from internal proposals have been rigorously excluded.
