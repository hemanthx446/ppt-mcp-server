### Executive Overview & Strategic Context

Jharkhand Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited (JBVNL) stands at an essential decision point regarding its enterprise resource planning (ERP) backbone. The utility currently maintains an unactivated SAP ECC 6.0 landscape carrying a 16-month transactional backlog, unreconciled store inventories, open purchase orders, and pending payroll policies. In parallel, SAP has slated mainstream maintenance for ECC 6.0 / Business Suite 7 to conclude by the end of December 2027.

Lumbini Elite Solutions presents a **two-option delivery blueprint and commercial model** formulated specifically for CXO review:

| Evaluation Metric | Option 1: Existing ECC Operationalisation | Option 2: S/4HANA Private Cloud Transformation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Target Go-Live Timeline** | **5 Months** | **12 Months** |
| **Post-Go-Live Support** | **12 Months** (Months 6–17) | **12 Months** (Months 13–24) |
| **Implementation Subtotal** | **₹360.60 Lakh** | **₹772.40 Lakh** |
| **12-Month Support Subtotal** | **₹266.70 Lakh** | **₹290.70 Lakh** |
| **10% Client Contingency Reserve** | **₹62.73 Lakh** | **₹106.31 Lakh** |
| **Total Indicative Services Budget** | **₹690.03 Lakh (~₹6.90 Crore)** | **₹1,169.41 Lakh (~₹11.69 Crore)** |
| **Central Specialists (Peak FTE)** | 16.25 FTE (@ ₹29.00L/month) | 19.25 FTE (@ ₹37.00L/month) |
| **Field Execution Staff** | 104 Personnel across 7 Areas, 15 Circles, 15 Stores (4 mos) | 104 Personnel across 7 Areas, 15 Circles, 15 Stores (6 mos) |
| **Field Staffing Cost** | ₹195.60 Lakh (@ ₹48.90L/month) | ₹293.40 Lakh (@ ₹48.90L/month) |

| **Clean-Core Extensibility** | Limited; legacy ABAP modifications | Cloud-native via **SAP BTP** & Fiori UX |

> **Executive Recommendation:** Approve an initial **4-Week Assessment Gate** (included within the implementation budget of either chosen option). This establishes the verifiable ground truth: inspecting database health, sizing the exact backlog, confirming circle deployment locations, and reconciling SAP cloud licensing against the JUSNL reference model before capital commitment.

  * *Core Operational Users:* 425 Users (5:1 ratio = 85 FUE)

  * *Total Working:* 134 FUE (rounded to 136 FUE).

* **8008783 · SAP Payroll Processing, Private Cloud:** 715 Users (Y1–Y3), scaling to 850 Users (Y4–Y5).
* **8017352 · SAP Log Service, Private Cloud:** Mandatory security & audit service (1 Tenant).
* **8019271 · Business Data Cloud (BDC) Core Capacity:** 640 Capacity Units (CU) Y2–Y3; doubled to 1,280 CU in Y4 for SAP Analytics Cloud.
* **8019709 · Customer-Managed Data Products:** Flat fee prerequisite for BDC from Year 2.
* **8020297 · Digital Access, Private Edition:** 10,000 Documents block for external non-SAP integrations.
* **8021436 · Cloud ERP Disaster Recovery (DR):** Standby private cloud DR from Year 2.

#### 2. Public Cloud & BTP Fabric
* **8016044 · SAP BTP Enterprise Agreement:** Cloud credits tier (₹10.93L – ₹65.60L) powering custom integrations.
* **8019544 / 8019164 · Joule Base & AI Units:** 25 ➔ 50 ➔ 100 ➔ 150 Capacity Units for generative Joule AI in Finance & HCM.
* **8016701 · SAP Learning Hub:** 2 User subscriptions from Year 2 for internal cadre enablement.
* **5 Critical BTP Extensions for JBVNL:**
  1. *Provident Fund (PF) Trustee Extension:* Custom employee PF ledger, interest accrual, and withdrawal workflows.
  2. *SCADA / Grid Integration:* Ingesting sub-station feeder telemetry into plant maintenance ledgers.
  3. *Supplier Collaboration Portal:* Digital PO acknowledgment, dispatch instruction (DI) tracking, and ASN generation.
  4. *Timesheet & Attendance Mobile App:* Frontline field operator shift logging and leave requests.
  5. *BTP Document Management:* Secure digital repository for store gate passes, consumer bills, and invoices.
