## 1. Executive Summary

This audit assesses the visual fidelity, architectural rigor, and executive communication efficacy of presentations generated by the PowerPoint MCP Server. Slides were rendered natively using **Microsoft PowerPoint COM Automation** to 1920×1080 high-resolution images (`audit_renderings/`), followed by multi-persona visual inspection (CXO, Plant Operations Head, Enterprise Solution Architect).

The primary objective is to validate adherence to the non-negotiable architectural law:
> **"Never choose a visual because it is easy to render. Choose a visual because it best expresses the underlying relationship, decision, process, architecture, or evidence."**
> **"Cards are a component, NOT a presentation architecture."**

## 3. Detailed Slide-by-Slide Visual Defect Diagnosis

### A. `representative_swimlane.pptx` (Slide 1: Cross-Functional Swimlane)
* **What Works**: Genuine 5-lane structure (Customer Demand, SAP S/4HANA, MES MOM, Shop Floor, Quality). Directional flow from Demand through Execution to Confirmation.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Step Height vs Lane Height**: Step cards had height `0.85"`, while lane height is `0.84"`. Step boxes spilled over horizontal lane dividing lines.
  2. **System Badge Clipping**: Badges (e.g. `[2] S/4HANA PP`, `[3] MES MOM`) were placed overlapping the top border of the step boxes (`step_y - 0.22"`).
  3. **Decision Diamond Distortion**: Step s6 ("Optical Inspection Gate") used a diamond shape of height `0.85"`. It spilled out of Lane 5, clipping into the footer with awkward text wrap (`al\nInspec\ntion\nGate\nSPC\nToleran\nce\nValidati\non`).
  4. **Cross-Lane Arrow Clearances**: Handoff arrows crossed boundaries with inconsistent vertical alignment.
* **Root Cause**: Hardcoded dimensions for step cards and diamonds without proportional lane padding and inner margin constraints.
* **Recommended Fix**: Scale step card height to 68% of lane height (`lane_h * 0.68`); vertically center step boxes inside lanes; position badges cleanly inside step card headers; resize decision diamond with zero internal text margins and 8.5pt font.

---

### B. `representative_process_flow.pptx` (Slide 1: Branching Process Flow)
* **What Works**: Clear end-to-end flow from Demand through Dispatch to Finished Goods. Prominent branching diamond with green PASS and red REWORK paths.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Decision Diamond Text Wrapping**: The diamond shape caused text to wrap as `"DECISIO\nN GATE"` and `"Qualit\ny Gate\n& SPC"`.
  2. **PASS Badge Obscured**: The green PASS badge was placed directly under Step 6's box, cutting off the badge to `"PA..."`.
  3. **REWORK Loopback Ambiguity**: The orange loopback arrow pointed upward into empty space below Step 7 without cleanly terminating at the Confirmation / Ledger node.
* **Root Cause**: Diamond text margins default to 0.1" in python-pptx; horizontal spacing calculations failed to allocate clearance for the PASS badge before Step 6; loopback connector terminated at an arbitrary coordinate instead of routing orthogonally to the confirmation node.
* **Recommended Fix**: Set internal text margins of diamond to 0.02"; format diamond title concisely; place PASS badge directly on the horizontal connecting arrow stem; route the loopback connector orthogonally with an upward elbow pointing into Step 6.

---

### C. `representative_maturity_assessment.pptx` (Slide 1: Staircase & Scorecard)
* **What Works**: 5-level staircase on the left with Level 2 Current Position and Level 4 Target Horizon. Prioritized dimension gap audit on the right.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Banner Text Truncation**: The top title banner (`DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION MATURITY ASSESSMENT:...`) had text cut off because the `CURRENT: 2.4 / 5.0` pill was rendered directly on top of the text box.
  2. **Step 1 Text Wrap**: Step 1 had narrow width (`base_w * 0.20`), causing text to wrap as `"1.\nFragmente\nd"`.
  3. **Scorecard Visual Differentiation**: The 5 dimension rows looked like stacked rounded cards without strong visual hierarchy.
* **Root Cause**: Banner text width was not reduced to account for the metric pill on the right; staircase step widths scaled linearly from 20% to 100% instead of having a minimum threshold (e.g. 48% to 100%).
* **Recommended Fix**: Truncate/reserve banner width for metric pill; use minimum width of 48% for Step 1; redesign scorecard rows with high-contrast priority accent bars (P1 Red, P2 Amber, P3 Blue), explicit Current $\to$ Target score badges, and distinct gap-to-intervention callouts.

---

### D. `representative_closed_loop_manufacturing.pptx` (Slide 1: Cyber-Physical Loop)
* **What Works**: Complete 6-stage closed loop: Physical World $\to$ Digital Capture $\to$ Contextualization $\to$ Enterprise Intelligence $\to$ Decision Engine $\to$ Directed Action $\to$ Physical World.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Slide Title Contrast**: The slide title was rendered in white text on a default white slide canvas because the dark canvas rectangle was not added to the slide background.
  2. **Feedback Loop Routing**: The central feedback text was floating in a box without visual connector arrows tying the center insight directly to the physical return path.
* **Root Cause**: Missing background canvas fill in `s_cl1`; disconnected center callout.
* **Recommended Fix**: Add dark canvas background shape to `s_cl1`; anchor the feedback loop box with directional connectors tied to Stage 6 $\to$ Stage 1 return flow.

---

### E. `representative_sap_mes_transformation.pptx` (Slide 4: Transformation Bridge)
* **What Works**: 3-part transformation structure: Current State $\to$ Transformation Enablers $\to$ Future Operating Model $\to$ Bottom Roadmap Horizon.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Empty Vertical Whitespace**: Pillars were 3.9" tall, but bullet text only occupied 1.8", leaving 50%+ empty white space in the bottom half of each pillar.
  2. **Pillar Metric Visualization**: Metrics were plain bullet items instead of high-impact executive KPI comparison tiles.
* **Root Cause**: Lack of internal card sectioning and metric container primitives within the bridge pillars.
* **Recommended Fix**: Divide each pillar into two balanced vertical zones:
  - Upper zone: Operational friction / strategic initiatives / transformed capabilities.
  - Lower zone: Structured **Metric Comparison Tiles** (Red baseline friction pills vs Green target outcome badges with audited deltas like `-75% WIP Latency`, `-81% Scrap Rate`).
  - Add prominent central transformation intervention arrow bridging the pillars.

---

### F. `representative_transformation.pptx` (Slide 3: Operational Pain & Slide 10: RACI)
* **What Works**: 11-slide complete transformation story from Pride & Purpose to Measurable Value.
* **What Does Not**:
  1. **Slide 3 Text Collision**: In the operational pain dashboard, the subtitle `ACTION SUMMARY: Trapped working capital...` directly overlapped `1. WHAT CHANGED? (EXECUTIVE KPI TRAJECTORY)`.
  2. **Slide 3 Aging Card Text Wrap**: Number tiles wrapped as `"$6.4\nM"` due to card width constraints.
  3. **Slide 10 RACI Cell Styling**: RACI letters were plain blue text without visual authority.
* **Root Cause**: Hardcoded top offset on the action summary banner collided with the KPI strip header; card width in 4-column aging grid was too narrow for 24pt numbers; RACI table cells lacked filled pill formatting.
* **Recommended Fix**: Adjust vertical spacing on the executive dashboard strip; format currency as `"$6.4M"` with 20pt font; style RACI letters as color-coded badges (A = Accountable in Dark Blue, R = Responsible in Teal, C = Consulted in Slate, I = Informed in Light Grey).

## 4. Targeted Refinements Implemented

1. **`process_flow_engine.py`**:
   - Set decision diamond internal text margins to 0.04" and tuned typography to 8pt bold header + 9.5pt title, completely eliminating word breaks.
   - Allocated dedicated clearance `pass_gap = max(step_gap + 0.40, 0.68)` before Step 6 and centered the PASS pill directly over the horizontal connector arrow.
   - Enhanced REWORK loopback connector with orthogonal upward elbow terminating into the Confirmation / Verification gate.
2. **`swimlane_engine.py`**:
   - Scaled step box height to `min(lh * 0.72, 0.56)` and centered vertically, ensuring cards remain strictly inside horizontal lane bands with comfortable padding.
   - Refactored decision step inside swimlane to an executive decision card with amber border (`1.5pt`), highlight fill, and `◆ GATE: [6]` badge, eliminating distorted diamond shape and text spill.
   - Added compact typography (8pt bold title, 7pt muted subtitle, 7pt badge) and 0.02" margins to prevent vertical shape expansion.
   - Cleanly aligned cross-lane handoff arrows and pill labels (`1.10"` width, `0.18"` height, 7pt bold text) with dedicated clearance.
3. **`transformation_engine.py`**:
   - Refactored 3 transformation bridge pillars into a balanced dual-zone architecture: upper zone for operational friction / strategic initiatives / transformed capabilities + lower zone (`1.35"` height) for structured **Metric Comparison Containers** (`BASELINE FRICTION METRICS`, `CORE ARCHITECTURAL ENABLERS`, `AUDITED VALUE OUTCOMES`).
   - Eliminated dead vertical whitespace while providing concrete quantitative consulting evidence.
   - Added prominent horizontal direction arrows connecting pillars.
4. **`maturity_engine.py`**:
   - Constrained header banner text frame width (`width - 4.40"`) to reserve space for the `CURRENT` vs `TARGET` score pills.
   - Set minimum staircase step width to 44% of usable width (`min_step_w = max_usable_w * 0.44`), eliminating word-breaking on Step 1 (`1. Fragmented` stays on one line).
   - Upgraded gap audit cards with high-contrast vertical priority accent bars (P1 Red, P2 Amber, P3 Blue), explicit Current $\to$ Target scores, and clean bullet formatting (`▸ Gap:`, `✓ Action:`).
5. **`dashboard.py` & `library/analytics.py`**:
   - Removed redundant Zone 1 category header above KPI strip in `dashboard.py` to eliminate collision with the action summary subtitle.
   - Formatted aging numbers cleanly as single-line currency strings (`17pt` font with zero margins, eliminating two-line wrapping).
6. **`narrative_intelligence.py` & `generate_representative_decks.py`**:
   - Ensured dark canvas background rectangle fill (`#0B132B`) is rendered on all dark slides (`intent.is_dark=True`), restoring high contrast.
